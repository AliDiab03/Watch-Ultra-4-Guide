Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Apple Watch Ultra 4 Guide app ("the App"), published by MOHA DEV ("we", "us"), handles information. The App is a free, unofficial guide for Apple Watch owners.

Information we collect

We do not require an account and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. We do not run our own servers that store user data.

The App saves one small setting on your device (whether you have finished the welcome screens). It never leaves your device.

Advertising

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use certain data, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. This is why the App requests the internet permission and the advertising ID permission.

In regions where consent is required (such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the App shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform so you can choose how your data is used for ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Offline content

The guide content and PDF manual are stored inside the App. Reading them does not send any information about you to us.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will help resolve it.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not collect it. Data handled by Google AdMob is governed by Google's policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the date updated above.
